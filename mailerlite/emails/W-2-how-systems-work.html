If you've been curious about hair systems but never quite understood them, here's the simple version — no jargon, no sales angle. There are only four parts that matter: the base, the hair, the attachment, and the routine. Everything else is detail.

Lace
Most natural at the hairline, most breathable, shortest life.
Poly (skin)
Most durable, easiest to clean and reattach, slightly less invisible up close.
Hybrid
Lace front for the hairline, poly at the sides and back. Most people end up here.
01 The hair — hand-tied into the base, matched to your colour, density and length. The goal is proportional and natural for your age, not "too perfect"
02 The attachment — tape or adhesive; properly done, it holds through workouts, swimming, wind and sleep
03 Daily — wash and style like natural hair (5 minutes)
04 Every 2–4 weeks — remove, clean scalp and base, reattach (30–45 minutes)
05 Every 3–6 months — replace the system
