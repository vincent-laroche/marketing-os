Hi {$name},

Three weeks in, most people ask the same question: what do I actually need to buy, and what's marketing?

The honest short list:
A dedicated adhesive remover
Not acetone, not household solvent. This is the one product where the cheap option genuinely damages the base.
A sulphate-free shampoo
Sulphates strip the knots and loosen the ventilation over time.
A soft-bristle looped brush
Standard brushes catch the knots and pull hair through the base.
A scalp prep wipe
Cheap, and it roughly doubles how long a bond holds.
That's it. You do not need a leave-in, a serum, or a heat protectant unless you style with heat.

If you already have equivalents that work, keep using them. I'd rather you had a system that lasts than a bigger order.

Founder, Hair Solutions Co.
