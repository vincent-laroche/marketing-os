02 · Hook Bank

STEAL FROM YOURSELF

Approved openers by pillar. Rotate; never reuse within 3 weeks. All follow the voice: no exclamation marks, no emoji, no hype.

Education

"This is not a wig." — pattern: blunt correction
"Will the hairline look real?" — pattern: the exact question they typed
"New here? Start with these 5 decisions." — pattern: orientation
"Density 80 / 100 / 130 — what each actually looks like." — pattern: honest comparison
"Why I don't recommend the cheapest system on the market." — pattern: costly honesty

Founder / Brand

"I wear what I sell." — pattern: skin in the game
"Before it ships, I check this." — pattern: standard as story
"Built to disappear." — pattern: product paradox
"No one has to know. That's the point." — pattern: discretion as value
"We don't sell hair. We sell mornings." — pattern: reframe the category

Proof (real quotes only)

"I forgot I was wearing it." — the gold standard of proof
"The first hairline that fooled my barber."
"Took me a year to try. Wish I'd done it sooner." — pattern: hesitancy reframe

Every proof post carries a consent record. No real quote, no post — swap in education instead.

Product

"[Name], photographed honestly." — pattern: anti-marketing
"[Name], up close." — pattern: macro respect
"The everyday one." — pattern: role, not specs
"The unsung hero." — pattern: role, not specs

One product per post. Specs in the caption, never crowding the tile.

03 · Caption Frameworks

PLUG AND PLAY

Education post

  • Line 1 — restate the hook plainly
  • Middle — 2–4 short sentences, one idea each; no lists of five things
  • Close — save prompt or specific DM keyword ("DM BASE")
  • 6–8 hashtags from Bundle A

Product post

  • Line 1 — name + one-line role ("Mono Pro. Silent, breathable, structured.")
  • Middle — base, density options, honest trade-off
  • Close — "Specs and colors → link in bio" + UTM
  • Bundle B tags

Founder / BTS post

  • Line 1 — first person, present tense
  • Middle — one concrete detail (the swatch, the 6am light, the box)
  • Close — "DM me — I read every one."
  • Bundle C tags
● DM keywords (the quiet funnel)
  • START new-buyer guide
  • BASE base chooser
  • COLOR free color check (daylight photo)
  • FIT fit questions
  • QC inspection checklist
  • HAIRLINE realism guide
  • PROOF reviews

Every keyword gets a saved reply written in the brand voice — set all seven up before Aug 3.

05 · Measurement & Guardrails

CHECK MONTHLY

What to measure (one axis at a time)

  • Reach engine — reel views + non-follower reach % (reels are the growth lever)
  • Trust — saves on education carousels; profile visits after founder posts
  • Funnel — DM keyword volume per week; link-in-bio clicks (UTM: rebrand-launch-2026)
  • Conversion — code redemptions FIRST10 / FALL15; Shopify sessions from IG
  • Monthly reviewDay 30Day 60Day 90Double down on the top pillar; do not add new ones.

Never (the standing list)

  • No emoji or exclamation marks in feed copy
  • No engagement bait
  • No giveaways
  • No medical claims
  • No guaranteed-transformation language
  • No fabricated testimonials — real, consented sources only
  • No invented product, refund, or shipping claims — live store policy is the only source
  • No discount stacking — two promos per 90 days is the ceiling
  • No reposting old OneHead content — clean slate means clean slate
● If a week collapses (it will)

Priority order when time runs out:

  1. Daily story
  2. Scheduled feed post
  3. Reel

A missed reel is a gap; a broken daily streak is a signal. Fillers from the quote bank can hold any feed slot for 48 hours.
