LOCKED

The chosen design system: warm cream paper, coral sun, ochre accents, the ceramic mannequin bust wearing real hair, torn-paper and stone textures, big serif statements with italic emphasis, and small technical mono metadata in the margins. Editorial atelier — never ecommerce.

Motifs (use 1–2 per asset, never all)

  • Coral sun — one large textured circle, off-center, behind the subject
  • The bust — ceramic mannequin head wearing a real hair system (our signature — replaces Atelier Zero's broken Greek bust)
  • Torn paper + cracked stone — collage edges, one corner only
  • Dotted grid + thin circles — technical margin drawings
  • Ochre blot — small painted accent, never dominant
  • Margin metadata — vertical mono text: series no., issue, date

Typography & format rules (carried over)

  • Display serif 64–96pt, italic for the emphasized word, bold sans for hard statements
  • Sans/mono minimum 22pt at export
  • Wordmark on every asset (top-left or top-center)
  • Feed 1080×1080 with an 80px safe area
  • Story 1080×1920 with 250px top + 280px bottom reserved
  • Reel cover 1080×1920 with type centered for the 1080×1350 feed crop
  • One idea per asset. The product, the man, or the message — never all three
  • Ink tiles are contrast moments only — no two adjacent in any 9-tile view
● The 9-tile check (after Week 1, every Saturday)

Pull the most recent 9 tiles.

  • No two ink tiles touching
  • At least one cream typographic tile per row
  • One product moment and one human/bust moment in every 9

If it fails, swap the next post's archetype. Week 1 is exempt (locked tile-by-tile).

Reference folders stay: Atelier Zero originals + mannequin swaps + backgrounds = the mood board for every shoot.

04 · Voice & Guardrails

NON-NEGOTIABLE

Voice

  • Grown, quiet, precise. Short sentences. No hype.
  • First person from Vincent — "I inspect every order" — the founder is the proof
  • Understatement over superlatives: "photographed honestly" beats "stunning"
  • The mission line: "For the man who wants to look like himself again."

Hard guardrails (from the Week 1 batch — now global)

  • No exclamation marks
  • No emoji in feed copy
  • No medical claims
  • No guaranteed-transformation language
  • No invented product, refund, or shipping claims
  • No fabricated testimonials — ever. Every quote/before-after post ships only with a real, consented source. Slots are flagged in the calendar.
  • No engagement bait (“tag a friend”, “drop a 🔥”)
  • No reposting old OneHead content
  • Product names, specs, and prices verified against the live Shopify catalog before posting
