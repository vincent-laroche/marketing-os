# 2026 Rebrand Launch

This is the retained active campaign package for the Hair Solutions Co. social rebrand launch beginning
August 3, 2026.

## Authority and use

- Notion's Content Production Board remains the operational owner for dates, status, captions, asset
  links, and publishing readiness.
- `campaign.snapshot.json` is the versioned repository snapshot of the full 90-day calendar (104
  scheduled rows), the detailed 30-day creative plan (44 feed items and 98 Story frames), and the weekly
  Story rhythm.
- The numbered HTML and spreadsheet files are review and production-support artifacts. They are not
  permission to publish or schedule.
- Product names, specifications, prices, availability, offers, customer proof, and consent must be
  checked against their live owner immediately before production or scheduling.
- Clearly labeled production holds are intentional. Do not replace them with invented testimonials,
  generated imagery, stock photography, or assumed business facts.

## Retained artifacts

1. `01-master-strategy.html` — campaign strategy and operating logic.
2. `02-day-by-day-wireframes.html` — complete day-by-day production map.
3. `03-content-calendar.xlsx` — working calendar for spreadsheet review.
4. `04-social-playbook.html` — publishing, voice, and operating playbook.
5. `05-30-day-creative-draft.html` — review gallery with non-production holds.
6. `campaign.snapshot.json` — structured, versioned 90-day calendar plus the detailed 30-day creative
   plan and Story rhythm.

## Template source

Reusable production structures live under `../../templates/`. Campaign files may reference those
patterns, but must not become a second template system.
