Most systems don't wear out. They get worn out — by advice that sounds sensible and isn't. Half the "tips" circulating in forums cost you months of lifespan.

Here are the ones we see doing the most damage, next to what actually happens.

{{ image: two systems after 4 months — one cared for correctly, one washed daily with the wrong products }} Same system, same age. The difference is entirely routine.
 
[Comparison] — myth vs reality "Wash it daily like real hair" — Daily washing strips the knots. 2–3 times a week with a sulfate-free wash is the honest ceiling. "More adhesive = stronger hold" — More adhesive means more residue, harder removal, and a shorter base life. Thin, even coverage holds better. "Any remover works" — Alcohol-heavy removers dry the base until it cracks. Use one matched to your adhesive. "Brush it into place when wet" — Wet hair pulls at the knots hardest. Pat dry, then style. "Sleep prep is optional" — Friction is the silent killer. A minute of prep (or a smoother pillowcase) buys real weeks.

None of this adds routine time. It just replaces the wrong five minutes with the right five.

How often should I deep-clean the base?
Roughly monthly — residue build-up, not dirt, is what usually forces early retirement. Can a barber help with upkeep? — Yes — more work with systems than you'd expect. Cutting it dry while worn is the norm. What single change buys the most lifespan? — The remover. It's the cheapest fix on this list and the most commonly wrong.
