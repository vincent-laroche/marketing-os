The most common question we get, by a wide margin: which base should I choose?

Most answers you'll find online are really product pitches. Here's the version that isn't.

There is no best base. There's only the trade-off, and it's the same one every time: the more realistic a base looks, the thinner and more fragile it is. Everything else — brand names, marketing terms, price tiers — sits downstream of that single trade-off.

So the useful question isn't "what's best?" It's "which do I care about more: the most natural possible hairline, or a system that survives my life?"

{{ image: close-up comparison of base constructions at the hairline }} Left: Swiss lace at the hairline. Right: skin at the same angle. Both read as scalp from conversation distance — the difference is what each costs you in lifespan and routine.
 
Lace (Swiss, French) — most natural hairline, breathable, scalp shows through the mesh. 3–6 months.
Skin (Nano 0.03mm → Dura 0.12mm) — clean shaved-scalp illusion, easiest to attach and remove. 1–8 months depending on thickness.
Mono (Mono Pro) — most durable, natural movement, comfortable for daily wear. 5–8 months.

A rough rule that holds up: if you're new and nervous about detection, start lace at the front. If you're active, sweat a lot, or want the simplest routine, start skin or hybrid. If you want maximum life per unit, Mono Pro.

Hybrid (Lace Edge, Mono Fusion LF, Thin Skin LF) — lace hairline with a PU perimeter for secure bonding. 3–6 months.
