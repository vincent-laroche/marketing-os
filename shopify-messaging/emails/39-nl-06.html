Here's the mistake almost everyone makes on their first order: they buy more hair than they ever had.

It's understandable. You've been missing hair for years — of course "full" sounds right. But a 40-year-old with the density of a 20-year-old doesn't read as lucky. It reads as wrong, even to people who can't say why. Density is the #1 reason systems get noticed — not the base, not the colour. The amount of hair.

How to think about it:

  • Match the hair you'd have if you'd never lost it — at your age, not at 20. That's the whole rule.
  • Density is graded, not flat. A believable system runs lighter at the hairline and temples, fuller through the mid and crown. A flat, uniform density is what makes a front look like a wall.
  • Rough ladder: 60–65% reads light and mature · 70–80% is the natural range for most men 30–50 · 90%+ only holds up on younger faces — and even then, graded.
  • When in doubt, go one step lighter. You can add density on the next order; you can't subtract it from this one.
Can density be adjusted after I order?
Not on the unit itself; a barber can thin it slightly, but it's better specced right. Most customers dial it in by order two. What do most men actually order? — Medium (70–80%).
