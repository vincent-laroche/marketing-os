Week 1: getting acquainted.

Hi {{ personalization_token("contact.firstname", "there") }}, your first week with a hair system is mostly about observation. You’re learning how it feels, how it moves, and how your scalp responds.

Focus on these three things this week:

  • Bonding: Don’t wash or wet the system in the first 24–48 hours after application. This gives the adhesive time to cure properly.
  • Scalp care: Keep the perimeter clean and dry. If you feel any irritation, let it breathe — don’t re-apply adhesive on irritated skin.
  • Hair care: Use a gentle, sulfate-free shampoo when you first wash. Avoid heat styling on high settings until you’ve tested how the hair responds.

Most first-week questions come down to adhesive and washing. If you’re unsure about anything, reply and ask. No question is too basic.
