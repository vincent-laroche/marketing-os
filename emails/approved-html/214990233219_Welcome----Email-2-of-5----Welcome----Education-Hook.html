One of the most common questions we hear from people exploring hair systems for the first time is: "Which base type is right for me?"

Here's a quick breakdown to help you decide:

🔹 Lace Base
The most natural-looking option. The fine mesh creates an invisible hairline that blends seamlessly with your skin. Best for: front hairline visibility, natural appearance priority.

🔹 Poly (Polyurethane) Base
Durable and easy to maintain. A thin, transparent layer that's simple to attach and clean. Best for: longevity, easy maintenance, active lifestyles.

🔹 Hybrid/Mono Base
Combines the best of both worlds — lace front for a natural hairline with a poly or mono back for durability. Best for: balanced appearance and durability.
