I know that first application can feel a bit nerve-wracking, so here's a quick-start guide to get you going:

Step 1: Unbox and inspect
Carefully remove the system from packaging. Check that the color, density, and base match your order. If anything looks off, reply to me immediately.

Step 2: Prepare your scalp
Clean the application area with scalp cleanser. Make sure the skin is completely dry and oil-free.

Step 3: Apply adhesive
Apply your chosen adhesive (tape or liquid) to the base perimeter. For tape, apply strips to the system. For liquid, apply a thin layer and let it get tacky.

Step 4: Position and press
Start at the front hairline and gently press back. Use a mirror to align. Press firmly for 30 seconds across the entire base.

Step 5: Style
Cut, style, and blend as needed. Your system can be cut by any barber or stylist.
