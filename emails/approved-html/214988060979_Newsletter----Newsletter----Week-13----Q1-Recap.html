Q1 IN REVIEW. Q2 ON DECK.

What we built, what you loved, and what's next.

Another quarter in the books — and what a quarter it was. From new product launches to community milestones, here's everything that happened at Hair Solutions Co. and a look at where we're headed next.

What launched. This quarter we introduced new French lace and Swiss lace systems in expanded color ranges, including natural gray blends that our customers have been requesting for months. We added three new poly base options optimized for warm climates, and our first hybrid system with a reinforced mono crown hit the store to strong reviews. On the care side, we launched a complete starter bundle that gives new customers everything they need in one box — adhesive, remover, shampoo, conditioner, and tools.

What you loved most. Our best-selling system continued to dominate, but the real story of Q1 was the surge in care product orders. More of you are investing in proper maintenance routines, and it shows — our customer support team reports that system longevity questions have decreased while satisfaction scores have climbed. The educational content on base types was our most-opened email series ever, and the customer stories feature drove record engagement. You asked for real talk, and we delivered.

Community milestones. We crossed a meaningful threshold this quarter — thousands of systems shipped to customers across North America and beyond. Our review average sits at 4.8 stars. And the messages we receive daily from men who say their confidence has changed — those never get old. Every single one reminds us why this work matters.

What's coming in Q2. Next quarter brings new product categories we've been developing behind the scenes, expanded customization options, and a refreshed website experience designed to make finding your perfect system even easier. We're also building out more educational content based on your feedback — including video guides for self-application and maintenance. Stay tuned. The best is ahead.

Thank you for being part of the Hair Solutions Co. community. Whether you joined this week or you've been with us from the beginning — we're building this for you.
