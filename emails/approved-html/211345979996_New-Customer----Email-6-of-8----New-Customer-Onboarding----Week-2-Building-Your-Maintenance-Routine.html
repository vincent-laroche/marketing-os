Week 2: settling in.

Hi {{ personalization_token("contact.firstname", "there") }}, if you’ve made it through your first week, you’re past the steepest part of the learning curve. Week two is about building a routine that fits your life.

What to establish this week:

  • Wash schedule: Most wearers find a rhythm of every 5–10 days, depending on activity level and scalp type. Find what works for yours.
  • Re-application: If you’re using tape or glue, this is the week to practice your removal and re-application process. Go slowly — speed comes with repetition.
  • Products: Keep your product lineup simple for now. Scalp protector before application, gentle shampoo for washing, light conditioner or leave-in for the hair. Nothing heavy near the base edges.

By the end of this week, you should have a basic rhythm. It won’t be perfect — that’s normal. The goal is consistency, not perfection.
