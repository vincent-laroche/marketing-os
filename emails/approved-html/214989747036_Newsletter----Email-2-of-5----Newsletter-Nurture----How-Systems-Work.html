If you’re newer to hair replacement systems, here’s a quick rundown of how it all works.

A system is a custom base — made from materials like lace, polyurethane (poly), or a combination — onto which human hair is hand-tied to match your natural color, density, and growth pattern.

It’s attached to your scalp using adhesive or tape, sitting over the area where hair has thinned, and blends with your existing hair around it.

With proper care, a quality system can last several months before needing replacement, and most people find the day-to-day routine becomes second nature within a couple of weeks.
