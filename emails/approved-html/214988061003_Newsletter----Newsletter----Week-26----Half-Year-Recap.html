H1 2026 — THE RECAP.

The milestones, the growth, and what is next.

Six months ago we started the year with a simple goal: make the best hair replacement systems available and make every customer feel like they made the right choice. As we close out the first half of 2026, we want to share what that goal has looked like in practice — and thank the people who made it happen.

By the numbers: We shipped over 12,000 orders in H1. We launched 7 new hair systems, including the Meridian Lace and Apex Hybrid that became instant best sellers. Our customer community grew to over 3,500 active members. And our satisfaction rate held at 96% — the highest it has ever been.

The products you loved most: Our top sellers this year were the Apex Hybrid (our most versatile system), the Stealth Poly Ultra-Thin (for the experienced wearers who want invisible), and our Maintenance Essentials Bundle (because great care products make great systems last longer). We also saw a surge in color sample ring orders, which tells us you are getting more intentional about finding the perfect match — and we love that.

What is coming in H2: Without giving too much away — new base technologies, expanded color options including more refined gray blending, a refreshed website experience, and loyalty rewards we have been designing specifically for our repeat customers. The second half of this year is going to be significant.

None of this happens without you. Every order, every question you ask our support team, every review you leave, and every friend you tell about us contributes to what this company is becoming. Hair Solutions Co. started as a belief that men deserve better options — and you are proving that belief right every single day.

Here is to an even stronger second half. We are just getting started.
