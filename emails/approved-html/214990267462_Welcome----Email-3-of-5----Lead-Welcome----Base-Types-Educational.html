One of the most important choices in a hair system is the base — the foundation everything else is built on.

Lace bases are lightweight, breathable, and offer the most natural-looking hairline, but require a bit more care. Poly (polyurethane) bases are thinner, more durable, and grip well, often preferred for active lifestyles. Many systems use a combination of both, balancing realism with durability.

There’s no single “best” option — it depends on your skin sensitivity, activity level, climate, and how natural you need the hairline to look in different lighting.

If you’re ever unsure which is right for you, our team is happy to talk through the trade-offs based on your specific situation.
