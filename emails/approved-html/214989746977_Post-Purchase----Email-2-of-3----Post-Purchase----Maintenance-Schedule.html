Now that you've been wearing your system for about a week, I wanted to share the maintenance schedule that keeps your system looking fresh and lasting as long as possible.

📅 Every 5-7 days: Quick Wash

  • Wash with sulfate-free shampoo
  • Light conditioner (avoid the base area)
  • Air dry when possible — low heat if needed

📅 Every 2-4 weeks: Reattachment

  • Remove the system carefully with adhesive remover
  • Clean your scalp and the base thoroughly
  • Apply fresh adhesive and reattach

📅 Every 30 days: Deep Clean

  • Full removal and deep conditioning treatment
  • Inspect the base for any wear
  • Check your adhesive supply — time to restock?

📅 Every 60-90 days: Professional Check

  • Consider a professional trim and restyle
  • Evaluate if the system needs replacement (typical lifespan: 3-6 months depending on base type)
