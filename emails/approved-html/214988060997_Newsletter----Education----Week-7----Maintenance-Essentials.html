Daily, weekly, and monthly — the guide that extends system life.

A great hair system deserves a great care routine. The difference between a system that lasts four weeks and one that lasts eight often comes down to how you maintain it. Here's the complete breakdown — daily, weekly, and monthly — so your system always looks and feels its best.

Daily care (2 minutes). Keep it simple. Use a wide-tooth comb or soft-bristle brush to gently style your system each morning. Avoid pulling or tugging at the base. If you use styling products, choose water-based formulas that won't build up residue on the base material. At night, a light brush-through before bed prevents tangling and keeps the hair aligned.

Weekly maintenance (15 minutes). Once a week, give your system a proper wash. Use a sulfate-free shampoo designed for hair systems — standard shampoos can strip the hair and degrade adhesive bonds. Follow with a lightweight conditioner, focusing on the mid-lengths and ends. Pat dry with a microfiber towel and let it air dry when possible. This is also the time to check your perimeter bond and touch up any edges that have started to lift.

Monthly refresh (30-45 minutes). Once a month, do a full removal, clean, and reattach. Remove the system using a citrus-based adhesive remover. Clean the base thoroughly to remove all adhesive residue. While the system is off, clean your scalp and let your skin breathe. Reapply with fresh adhesive or tape for a bond that feels like day one.
