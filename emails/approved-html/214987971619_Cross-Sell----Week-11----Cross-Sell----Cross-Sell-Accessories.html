The products that make your system last twice as long.

You've got the system. Now let's make sure you have everything you need to keep it looking flawless, feeling secure, and lasting as long as possible. The right accessories aren't optional — they're the difference between a good experience and a great one.

Adhesive: your foundation. Whether you prefer tape for convenience or liquid adhesive for a custom bond, the right adhesive is everything. Our professional-grade options are designed specifically for hair system bases — they hold strong in heat, humidity, and sweat, and they release cleanly when it's time for maintenance. Pro tip: lace bases typically pair best with liquid adhesive for a seamless front, while poly bases work beautifully with both tape and liquid.

Remover: the clean break. Never force a system off your head. A quality citrus-based remover dissolves adhesive gently, protecting both the base material and your scalp. Apply, wait two minutes, and the system lifts away cleanly. Using the right remover also prevents residue buildup that can shorten your system's lifespan. This is one product where quality directly impacts how long your system lasts.

System-safe shampoo and conditioner. Regular drugstore shampoo is formulated for natural scalps — not hair system bases. Sulfates and harsh detergents can degrade lace, weaken adhesive bonds, and dry out the hair. Our system-safe formulas clean effectively while preserving the integrity of the base and keeping the hair soft, manageable, and natural-looking wash after wash.

Styling and maintenance tools. A wide-tooth comb, a ventilated brush, a microfiber towel, and a quality leave-in conditioner round out the essentials. These aren't luxury items — they're the tools that keep your system performing at its best between maintenance sessions. Think of your kit as an investment that pays dividends every single day you wear your system. Get set up once, and you're covered for months.
