This week, let’s talk about one of the most common questions we get: lace vs. poly bases — what’s the difference, and which is right for you?

Lace bases are thin, breathable, and create the most natural-looking hairline, especially up close. They tend to require more frequent maintenance and are best suited to those willing to put in a bit more care.

Poly (polyurethane) bases are more durable, grip the scalp more securely, and hold up well to sweat and activity — a popular choice for active lifestyles, though the hairline can look slightly less natural in very close-up lighting.

Many of our clients land on a hybrid: lace at the front hairline for realism, poly through the rest of the base for durability. If you’re not sure which fits your life best, our team is happy to talk it through.
