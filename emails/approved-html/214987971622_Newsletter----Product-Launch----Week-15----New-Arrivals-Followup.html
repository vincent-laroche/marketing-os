Real first impressions and how they compare.

Last week we introduced three new hair systems — the Meridian Lace, Apex Hybrid, and Stealth Poly Ultra-Thin. Since then, early orders have shipped and the first impressions are coming in.

One customer told us the Meridian Lace is "the first system where I genuinely forget I am wearing it." Another said the Apex Hybrid gave him the confidence to start wearing systems full-time after months of hesitation. That is exactly why we developed these — to close the gap between wearing a system and forgetting you are.

How do they compare to the rest of our lineup? The Meridian sits alongside our existing full-lace options but with a noticeably thinner base and improved knot sealing. The Stealth Poly is our thinnest poly system to date, designed for experienced wearers who prioritize an invisible hairline above all else.
