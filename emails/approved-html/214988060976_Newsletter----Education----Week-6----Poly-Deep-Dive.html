POLY. BUILT TO PERFORM.

Durability meets a flawless, clean finish.

When it comes to durability, ease of use, and a consistently clean look, poly (thin skin) base systems are in a class of their own. If you value a low-maintenance routine and a polished appearance, this is the deep dive you've been waiting for.

What makes poly different? Poly bases are made from a thin, transparent polyurethane material that sits flush against your scalp. Because the base is smooth and non-porous, adhesive bonds are stronger and more consistent. The result is a secure hold that lasts longer between maintenance sessions — ideal for men with active schedules who don't want to think about their system throughout the day.

The clean, defined look. One of the standout benefits of poly systems is the seamless edge they create. Where lace mimics scalp through transparency, poly achieves its realism through a skin-like finish that blends into your own skin tone. This makes poly especially effective for men who wear their hair slicked back, parted cleanly, or in any style where the perimeter of the system is visible.

Easy attachment. Easy removal. Poly systems are the most beginner-friendly when it comes to self-application. The smooth surface accepts tape and liquid adhesive evenly, and removal is straightforward with the right solvent. If you're new to hair systems and want the simplest possible routine, poly gives you confidence from day one.

Who should choose poly? Poly is perfect for men who want maximum hold, minimal fuss, and a refined finish. It's the go-to for business professionals, athletes, and anyone who needs their system to perform without compromise.
