Top 5 Maintenance Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)

After thousands of customer conversations, the same mistakes keep coming up. Here are the five that shorten system life and weaken bonds the most:

1. Skipping scalp prep. Applying adhesive over residue or oils is the number one reason bonds fail early. Every attachment should start with a clean, dry scalp. 2. Using too much adhesive. More glue does not mean a stronger bond. It means a messier removal and more residue buildup. Thin, even coats are always better. 3. Ignoring the perimeter. The edges of your system take the most stress. If you are not pressing and sealing the perimeter after every wash, you are inviting lift. 4. Washing with the wrong products. Sulfate-heavy shampoos strip the hair and degrade adhesive bonds. Switch to a sulfate-free, system-safe shampoo. 5. Waiting too long between maintenance. Regular 2-3 week maintenance cycles keep your system looking fresh and your scalp healthy. Stretching it to 4-5 weeks causes buildup and reduces the life of the unit.

Seasonal Care Note: As we move into warmer months, increase your wash frequency slightly and consider switching to a water-based adhesive if you are not already using one. Heat and humidity break down solvent-based glues faster.
