Color codes, gray blending, and samples explained.

Getting the right color is just as important as getting the right base — and it is the area where most first-time buyers feel the least confident. Hair system color charts can look overwhelming, but once you understand the logic behind them, choosing becomes straightforward.

Understanding Color Codes: Most hair system colors use a numbering system. #1 is jet black, #1B is off-black, #2 is darkest brown, and the numbers go up through medium brown (#4), light brown (#6), dark blonde (#8), and beyond. The key is that these numbers are standardized across the industry, so you can compare confidently. If you currently wear a #4 from any brand, a #4 from us will be in the same family.

Gray Percentage: This is where customization gets powerful. If you have natural graying, we can blend gray into your system at any percentage — 5%, 10%, 20%, all the way up. Matching your natural gray level is what makes a system look age- appropriate and completely undetectable. Our recommendation: aim for slightly less gray than your natural hair, since systems do not gray over time the way natural hair does.

Hair Density: Density refers to how thick the hair is across the system. Light, medium-light, medium, and medium-heavy are the standard options. Most men do best with medium-light to medium — it looks the most natural. Going too heavy is the most common mistake. A natural-looking density always beats a thick, obviously full head of hair.

Order a Sample: If this is your first order or you are switching colors, we strongly recommend ordering a color sample ring. It ships free, arrives in 2-3 days, and lets you match against your natural hair in your own lighting. It takes the guesswork out entirely.
