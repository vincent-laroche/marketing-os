Choosing a hair system base is the most important decision you will make — and it is the one most guys get wrong on their first try. Not because the information is not out there, but because most of it is vague or biased. Here is the straightforward breakdown.

Full Lace is the gold standard for natural appearance. The entire base is a fine lace mesh, which means air flows through freely and the hairline is virtually undetectable. The trade-off is durability — lace bases are more delicate and typically last 2-4 months with daily wear. Best for: guys who prioritize a natural look above all else and are comfortable with regular maintenance. Price range: mid to premium.

Poly (Skin) bases are thin polyurethane membranes that lay flat against your scalp. They create an incredibly secure bond, are easy to clean, and are the most beginner-friendly option. The trade-off is breathability — poly does not ventilate like lace, so it can feel warmer. Best for: guys who want a reliable, easy-to-manage system with a strong hold. Price range: entry to mid.

Hybrid bases combine two or more materials — typically a lace or mono top with a poly perimeter. You get the natural movement and breathability of lace where it is visible, plus the secure bonding surface of poly around the edges. Best for: guys who want the best balance of appearance, durability, and ease of use. This is our most popular category. Price range: mid to premium.
