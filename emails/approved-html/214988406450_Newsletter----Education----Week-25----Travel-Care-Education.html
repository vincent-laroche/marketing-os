YOUR SUMMER TRAVEL CARE GUIDE.

Heat, humidity, swimming — your summer game plan.

Summer travel season is here, and if you are wearing a hair system, a little preparation goes a long way. Whether you are heading to the beach, a destination wedding, or just a week away from your usual routine, here is how to keep your system looking sharp on the road.

Pack a travel care kit. You do not need your full maintenance setup — just the essentials. A small bottle of adhesive remover, a travel-sized sulfate-free shampoo, a light leave-in conditioner, a wide-tooth comb, and your adhesive of choice. We put together a pre-packed travel kit that covers all of this in TSA-friendly sizes, so you can toss it in your carry-on without thinking about it.

Humidity and heat management. High humidity weakens most adhesive bonds faster than normal conditions. If you are heading somewhere tropical, consider switching to a waterproof or humidity-resistant adhesive for the duration of your trip. Also, avoid leaving your system in direct sunlight for extended periods — UV exposure can shift the color over time, especially on lighter shades.

Swimming protocol. You absolutely can swim with a hair system, but preparation matters. Apply a waterproof adhesive at least 24 hours before swimming so the bond fully cures. After swimming — whether pool or ocean — rinse your system with fresh water as soon as possible. Chlorine and salt water are not system-friendly if left to sit.

Keep a backup bond. This is the tip most guys wish they had known sooner. Pack a small strip of tape or a single-use adhesive applicator. If your bond loosens while you are away from home, a quick perimeter touch-up takes five minutes and saves you from stress.

Travel should be about the experience, not about worrying over your hair. A little prep before you leave means total confidence while you are there.
