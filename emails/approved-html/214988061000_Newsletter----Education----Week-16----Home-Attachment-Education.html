YOUR FIRST HOME ATTACHMENT, SIMPLIFIED.

Step-by-step confidence — no salon needed.

One of the biggest questions we get from new customers is "can I really do this myself?" The answer is yes — and it is easier than you think. Thousands of our customers attach and maintain their systems entirely at home, saving time and money while staying in full control of their look.

Here is the process, broken down into five clean steps:

1. Prep your scalp. Remove any residue from your previous bond using a citrus-based adhesive remover. Clean the area with scalp cleanser and let it dry completely. A clean surface is the single biggest factor in how long your bond lasts.

2. Apply your adhesive. Whether you prefer tape or liquid adhesive, apply thin, even layers. For liquid glue, we recommend 3-4 light coats with 3 minutes of drying time between each coat. The surface should be tacky, not wet, before you place the system.

3. Position and press. Start at the front hairline and carefully align the system, pressing down from front to back. Use a rat-tail comb to check alignment before fully pressing the perimeter into place.

4. Bond and secure. Once positioned, press firmly around the entire perimeter using a folded cloth. Apply gentle heat with a blow dryer on low to activate the adhesive and lock the bond.

5. Blend and style. Cut in your desired style, blend the hairline with a fine-tooth comb, and use a light-hold styling product to finish. You are done.

The entire process takes 30-45 minutes once you have done it a few times. We put together a full video tutorial that walks you through each step in real time — it is the best way to build confidence before your first solo attachment.
