To help you get the maximum lifespan from your system, here are the care essentials:

Daily care:

  • Style gently — treat it like your natural hair
  • Avoid excessive heat (use low-medium settings on styling tools)
  • Protect from chlorine and salt water when swimming

Weekly maintenance:

  • Wash with sulfate-free shampoo every 5-7 days
  • Condition lightly — avoid the base area
  • Inspect your adhesive bond and touch up edges if needed

Monthly service:

  • Remove the system completely for a deep clean
  • Clean your scalp thoroughly
  • Reapply with fresh adhesive for the strongest hold

The right products make all the difference. Using the correct adhesive, tape, and cleanser for your base type protects your investment and extends the life of your system significantly.
