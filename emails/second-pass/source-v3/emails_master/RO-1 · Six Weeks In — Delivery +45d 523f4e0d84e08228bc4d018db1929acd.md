# RO-1 · Six Weeks In — Delivery +45d

Body: Hi {{ firstname }},

Six weeks is the point where a system starts telling you how it's going to age. Two things worth checking this week.

The front perimeter. Hold it to the light and look along the very front edge. Fine splits there are normal wear, but they spread. Catching one now is a repair; catching it in a month is a replacement.

The knots at the crown. If hair is shedding faster than it was, that's usually cleaning technique rather than a fault — too much friction on the base.

Where you are in the cycle: most systems run three to six months. At six weeks you're roughly a third through, which means now is the time to be maintaining, not replacing.

Reply with a photo if anything looks off and I'll tell you what I'm seeing.

Vincent
