Base types

Lace, poly, or hybrid

Lace — most natural at the hairline, most breathable, shortest life. Best if appearance is the priority and you don't mind replacing more often.

Poly — most durable, easiest to clean and reapply, slightly less invisible up close. Best if you want low maintenance and a long bond.

Hybrid — lace front for the hairline, poly at the sides and back. Most people end up here.
