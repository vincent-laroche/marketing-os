What's happening right now:

Every hair system we produce goes through a multi-step process that ensures quality and consistency:

Base construction — Your base is cut and prepared to your exact measurements. Whether it's lace, poly, or hybrid, each base material is selected for durability and comfort.

Hair selection — human hair is selected and color-matched to your specifications. We match for color, texture, and wave pattern.

Hand-tying — Hair is individually knotted into the base by skilled technicians. This is what creates the natural look and movement.

Finishing — The completed system is washed, styled, and inspected. Any unit that doesn't pass gets reworked.

This is why we don't rush production — the result is a system that looks and feels natural from day one.
