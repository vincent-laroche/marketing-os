# Migrated Social data

**Status: public historical/reference material; production quarantine applies.**

The spreadsheet and CSV in this directory were transferred into the public Marketing OS repository for traceability and planning reference. They are not an approved content library, current product catalogue, current pricing source, consent record, evidence register, or publication queue.

Claims, prices, offers, testimonials, customer or model references, before/after material, metrics, dates, and platform copy must be independently reviewed before reuse. Customer proof and customer media require an authoritative source, rights review, and exact-use consent outside this directory. A migrated row does not authorise posting, scheduling, paid promotion, or other platform action.

The owning audit is [Issue #102](https://github.com/vincent-laroche/marketing-os/issues/102), with its current classification and required disposition recorded in [`docs/social-media/PUBLIC-CONTENT-AUDIT.md`](../../docs/social-media/PUBLIC-CONTENT-AUDIT.md).
