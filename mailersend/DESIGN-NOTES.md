Three transactional templates built 2026-08-19 from the Figma **Email Design System**
(`9Il504CQE8jLaUTBVzphqc`), canvas `225:357`:

| Template | Figma frame | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `SVC-1-order-confirmed.html` | `284:21673` — Transactional V2, *Structured & Detailed* | Order confirmation |
| `SVC-2-specification-review.html` | `284:21682` — Transactional V3, *Branded & Warm* | Production update / specification review |
| `SVC-3-reorder-received.html` | `284:21691` — Transactional V4 | Reorder confirmation |

They are generated by `build_service_emails.py`. **Edit that script, never the HTML** —
the files are build output and are overwritten. `--check` fails if they have drifted.

## Where the templates deliberately depart from the Figma frames

Every one of these is a rule collision, not a preference. Listed so nobody
"fixes" them back.

**1. No pure-white surfaces.** The comps draw the pre-header, navigation and divider
cards in `#FFFFFF` and the content cards in `#F7F1DE`, giving a white-band / cream-card
rhythm. Pure white is not a permitted email section surface, and the palette that governs
email colour is the rendered Atelier Zero modules (AGENTS.md #1), not the Figma fills.
Every card therefore takes the one light card tone `#F6EFD9`, and the
separation is carried by the 1px `#C7BFAC` hairline plus the role radius — 8px nav, 16px
divider and footer, 20px title/content/product. Cards stack flush with `padding:0 12px`
and no vertical gap, so the meeting corners notch and the stack still reads as stacked
boxes. V4 keeps its coral pre-header banner; that is the one accent block.

**1b. No page background at all.** Beyond the white-surface question, the email paints no
wallpaper: `<body>` and the outer wrapper are transparent, so the client's own background
shows through the 12px gutter. Hard rule set 2026-08-19 by Vincent (AGENTS.md #5) and applied
to every email in this repo, both platforms. On a dark-mode client the gutter goes dark while
the cards stay light — intended, not a bug.

**2. No unsubscribe link.** The comp footers carry *Unsubscribe* and *Manage Preferences*.
These are service emails about an order the customer placed — there is nothing to opt out
of, and offering it would imply order updates can be switched off. The link slot keeps the
comp's shape and carries *Order status*, *Contact us* and *Privacy policy* instead, above
the same service-mail notice the earlier MailerSend templates use.

**3. Real postal address.** The comp prints `FOREST HILLS, NEW YORK 11209`. Every shipped
Hair Solutions Co. email uses the operating address, `Ehitajate tee 110, Tallinn, Estonia`.

**4. Totals card added to V2 and V4.** Not in the comps. Both show per-line prices; an
order confirmation that prices each line and never totals them is a support ticket. The
card reuses the content-card geometry and collapses nothing else.

**5. Care-product content is data-driven, not literal.** The comps name *Citrus Release
Cleanser*, *Scalp Preparation Spray*, *Lace Bond Adhesive*, *Lace Cleanser*, *Bond Remover*
and *Scalp Tape*. **None of those exist in the catalogue** — `products.json` carries hair
systems, order add-ons and services only, and the add-ons have no images. The
recommendation grids therefore loop over whatever `recommendations` / `resources` the
payload supplies and the whole card collapses when the list is empty. The fixtures use
real catalogue products and real guide pages. When consumables ship, populate the payload;
no template change is needed.

**6. Text social links, not glyph images.** V4's footer uses raster social icons. Text
links carry the same information, stay legible with images blocked, and add no hosted
assets.

**7. Fonts.** Figma specifies Inter Tight / Inter / JetBrains Mono / Playfair Display.
Those load from Google Fonts with the documented web-safe fallbacks
(`Helvetica Neue`/Arial, `Courier New`, Georgia). Only colour is governed by the module
previews; type follows Figma and the two earlier MailerSend templates.

## Layout facts worth not rediscovering

- **`CARD_W + 2 × GUTTER = 600`** — 576 + 2×12. Change one and the other follows.
- **The mobile breakpoint is 620px, not 600.** A desktop client whose reading pane is
  exactly 600px wide must still get the desktop layout; 620 leaves room for chrome.
- **`.az-col` is for grid columns only.** It stacks a cell on mobile. Putting it on a
  line-item thumbnail centres a 100px image over centred text and looks accidental — those
  cells stay a row and the thumbnail shrinks to 76px via `.az-thumb` instead.
- **`.az-grid` cancels the negative margin on mobile.** The grid tables use `margin:0 -6px`
  to pay for the cells' gutter padding; mobile zeroes that padding, so the margin has to go
  too or the stacked column sits 6px left of the card's text.
- **`display:block` images ignore the parent's `text-align`.** A centred logo needs
  `margin:0 auto` of its own.
- **Shopify CDN `.webp` originals need `&format=jpg`.** Outlook cannot decode WebP.
  `&width=`/`&height=`/`&crop=center` do the cropping server-side, so no `object-fit`
  is needed — email has none.
- **Grid arity is validated, not trusted.** Three-up (SVC-1, SVC-3) and two-up (SVC-2) are
  fixed widths; a fourth item would silently overflow the 576px card on desktop, where no
  media query rescues it, so `validate()` refuses it.
