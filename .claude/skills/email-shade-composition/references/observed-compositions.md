## What the set shows

**Ink is rare.** Only four of thirteen use an Ink content block, never more than three, never
consecutively beyond a header-plus-hero pair. It appears as a hero, a mid CTA, or a footer.

**Coral as a surface appears exactly once** — Welcome V2, a single block with ink text and an ink
pill. Confirming: one per email, never a header or footer.

**Seams bracket.** Where a block is a set-aside — trust strip, testimonial, product row — it
carries a seam above *and* below. Single seams mark one emphasis moment. Five of thirteen use
none at all, so seams are a tool, not a requirement.

**Transactional strips chrome.** No announcement bar in V1 and V2, no nav, no social, no marketing
CTA, footer reduced to legal links. V3 adds a coral bar and keeps it warm, which is the only
transactional variant that does.

**Two of the sixteen source files are unusable.** `Newsletter — Version 2 Editorial & Personal`
and `Newsletter — Version 3 Bold & Feature-Rich` exported at 180×37px. Those two compositions are
lost and would need re-exporting.
