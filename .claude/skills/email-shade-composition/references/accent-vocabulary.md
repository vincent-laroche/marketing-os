# Accent vocabulary

Devices that create variety *inside* a shade. Pick two or three per email. Four or more reads as
a sampler rather than a design.

## Type devices

- **Mixed-font headline** — a bold grotesque line followed by an italic serif line
  ("Talk to someone who *builds these.*"). The signature move; at most one per email.
- **Coral serif price** — right-aligned, in product rows and order lines.
- **Coral serif numerals** — `01 02 03` beside a numbered list.
- **Coral italic serif caption** — a short qualifier under a grid label ("Lighter finish").
- **Italic serif sign-off** — the company name set in serif italic under "Warmly,".

## Eyebrows — pick one per email

- **Coral em-dash eyebrow** — the default. Courier, uppercase, 2px tracking, `—` prefix.
- **Wordless coral glyph** — a single centred `★` in place of eyebrow text.
- **Outlined pill eyebrow** — the label inside a rounded outline chip.
- **Short coral rule** — a bare rule at the card's top edge, no text.

## Blocks and tiles

- **Dashed coral chip** — a code or spec token (`CODE: FIRSTATELIER`, `YOUR NEXT SYSTEM`).
- **Glyph circle tiles** — icon in a circle above a two-line bold label; trust and reassurance strips.
- **Glyph tiles** — a bare symbol per cell in a feature 3-up.
- **Numeral tiles** — a static countdown; inset panels, big numeral, mono unit label.
- **Nested sub-card** — a recessed inset for order summaries and product rows.

## Proof devices

- **Large coral quote mark** — opens a testimonial, centred above serif italic quote text.
- **Attribution line** — bold name over a mono uppercase qualifier (`VERIFIED CUSTOMER · NEW YORK`).
- **Author row** — circular avatar, bold name, mono meta line; editorial blocks.
- **Handwritten signature** — a signature image under the sign-off avatar.

## Links

- **Coral inline text link** — a bare coral word under a card title ("Read", "Shop").
- **Underlined legal links** — footer only.

## Rotation rule

Before choosing, check the previous campaign in the same series. If it used the same accent set,
change the set even when the mood stays the same. Rotation is what keeps a recurring series from
reading as one email sent repeatedly.
