Hi {{ firstname }},

If you've been curious about hair systems but never quite understood them, here's the simple version — no jargon, no sales angle. There are only four parts that matter: the base, the hair, the attachment, and the routine. Everything else is detail.

[Photo - Founder note]
I explain this to someone new nearly every day. The base is the biggest decision — it determines how natural the system looks, how breathable it feels, and how long it lasts. Get that right and the rest follows. — Vincent

[Comparison]
Lace — most natural at the hairline, most breathable, shortest life.
Poly (skin) — most durable, easiest to clean and reattach, slightly less invisible up close.
Hybrid — lace front for the hairline, poly at the sides and back. Most people end up here.

[Timeline: how it works]
1. The hair — hand-tied into the base, matched to your colour, density and length. The goal is proportional and natural for your age, not "too perfect"
2. The attachment — tape or adhesive; properly done, it holds through workouts, swimming, wind and sleep
3. Daily — wash and style like natural hair (5 minutes)
4. Every 2–4 weeks — remove, clean scalp and base, reattach (30–45 minutes)
5. Every 3–6 months — replace the system

No surgery, no chemicals, no downtime.
