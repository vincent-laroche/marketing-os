Hi {{ firstname }},

Every so often someone asks why a system takes 3–4 weeks when a wig ships tomorrow. Fair question. This email is the answer — a walk through what actually happens between your spec sheet and your door.

[Text - Section: how a system gets made]
1. The spec sheet becomes a pattern. Your base type, size and hairline shape get cut before any hair is touched. If the consultation flagged an irregular hairline or temple recession, it's built in here — not styled in later.

2. Colour is matched against a physical ring, not a photo. Screens lie. Grey percentage is counted per zone, because real grey doesn't distribute evenly.

3. The hair goes in by hand — knot by knot. Ventilation is the slow part. Direction matters: crown hair grows differently to hairline hair, and a system knotted flat looks combed even when it isn't. The hairline gets the finest knots and the lightest grading, because it's the part everyone sees first.

4. It gets checked against the spec, not against "looks fine." Density per zone, colour under two light sources, hairline placement. If it misses, it doesn't ship.

That's the whole reason for the wait. Nothing on a shelf, nothing pre-made and relabelled. A system built to a spec takes the time the spec takes.

Vincent Laroche
Founder, Hair Solutions Co.
