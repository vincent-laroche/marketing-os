“
I looked like me again. The version of me I’d been missing.
Before
Three years of minoxidil with minimal results. Expensive PRP treatments. Hats as a permanent accessory. “I stopped going to pool parties. I avoided photos. The worst part wasn’t the hair loss — it was how much mental space it took up.”
The decision
Months of research after finding hair systems on YouTube. “I was skeptical. I thought it would look fake, or that people would notice.” His first system: French lace, natural dark brown, medium density.
The first fit
“I put it on and just stared in the mirror.”
A year in
Girlfriend didn’t know for the first three months. Coworkers still don’t. Gym five days a week, swims on weekends, travels for work. “It just works. That’s the best thing I can say about it. It just works, and I get to live my life again.”
