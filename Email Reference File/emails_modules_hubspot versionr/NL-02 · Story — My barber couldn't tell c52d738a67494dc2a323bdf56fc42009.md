Every so often we ask a customer to tell their story properly — not a one-line review, but what actually happened: what they tried before, what they were afraid of, and what changed.

This one is Danny's. He's a 34-year-old project manager from Austin who started losing his hair at 26. Shared with permission.

[Quote - Centered]
"I looked like me again. The version of me I'd been missing."

[Testimonial]
Before: three years of minoxidil with minimal results. Expensive PRP treatments. Hats as a permanent accessory. "I stopped going to pool parties. I avoided photos. The worst part wasn't the hair loss — it was how much mental space it took up."

The decision: months of research after finding hair systems on YouTube. "I was skeptical. I thought it would look fake, or that people would notice." His first system: French lace, natural dark brown, medium density.

The first fit: "I put it on and just stared in the mirror."

Where he is now, a year in: girlfriend didn't know for the first three months. Coworkers still don't. Gym five days a week, swims on weekends, travels for work. "It just works. That's the best thing I can say about it. It just works, and I get to live my life again."
