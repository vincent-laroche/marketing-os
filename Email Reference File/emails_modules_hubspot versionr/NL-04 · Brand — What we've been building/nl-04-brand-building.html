Three Things,
Honestly Told
1 We tightened the consultation process. More of you are locking the right spec on the first order instead of the second — fewer adjustment rounds, faster reorders.
 
2 We expanded colour matching — including the grey blends customers kept asking for. If you were told “close enough” before, it’s worth re‑checking.
 
3 We rebuilt how we email you. Fewer sends, each one with an actual job. If we don’t have something useful to say, you won’t hear from us.
A note from me
The thing I’ve become most convinced of this year: the spec matters more than the product tier. A well‑specced mid‑range system beats a badly‑specced premium one every single time. It’s why we keep pushing consultations rather than upsells.
