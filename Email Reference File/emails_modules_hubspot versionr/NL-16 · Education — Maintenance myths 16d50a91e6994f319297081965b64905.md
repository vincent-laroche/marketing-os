[Comparison] — myth vs reality
"Wash it daily like real hair" — Daily washing strips the knots. 2–3 times a week with a sulfate-free wash is the honest ceiling.
"More adhesive = stronger hold" — More adhesive means more residue, harder removal, and a shorter base life. Thin, even coverage holds better.
"Any remover works" — Alcohol-heavy removers dry the base until it cracks. Use one matched to your adhesive.
"Brush it into place when wet" — Wet hair pulls at the knots hardest. Pat dry, then style.
"Sleep prep is optional" — Friction is the silent killer. A minute of prep (or a smoother pillowcase) buys real weeks.

None of this adds routine time. It just replaces the wrong five minutes with the right five.

[FAQ]
How often should I deep-clean the base? — Roughly monthly — residue build-up, not dirt, is what usually forces early retirement.
Can a barber help with upkeep? — Yes — more work with systems than you'd expect. Cutting it dry while worn is the norm.
What single change buys the most lifespan? — The remover. It's the cheapest fix on this list and the most commonly wrong.
