[Text - Section: what changed]
Three things, honestly told:

1. We tightened the consultation process. More of you are locking the right spec on the first order instead of the second, which means fewer adjustment rounds and faster reorders.
2. We expanded colour matching — including the grey blends customers kept asking for. If you were told "close enough" before, it's worth re-checking.
3. We rebuilt how we email you. Fewer sends, each one with an actual job. If we don't have something useful to say, you won't hear from us.

A note from me: the thing I've become most convinced of this year is that the spec matters more than the product tier. A well-specced mid-range system beats a badly-specced premium one every single time. It's why we keep pushing consultations rather than upsells.

Vincent Laroche
Founder, Hair Solutions Co.
